# Energy Carrier Color Palettes in Reports and Visualizations

This document compiles common color palettes used to represent **energy carriers** in major reports (IPCC, IEA, OWID, etc.).  
It provides references to figures, describes conventions, and suggests reusable palettes with example hex codes.

---

## 🔍 Evidence from Reports

| **Report / Source** | **Figure or Section** | **Energy Carriers / Types Shown** | **Colors Used / Implied** | **Remarks** |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| **IPCC AR6 WGIII SPM** ([SPM Fig. 3](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg3/downloads/report/IPCC_AR6_WGIII_SPM.pdf)) | Unit cost reductions and use in mitigation technologies | Fossil vs. Renewables | • Fossils: grey shading <br> • Renewables: blue lines | Demonstrates “clean vs fossil” separation. |

Sometimes aggregates categories. |

## ✅ Notes

- **No standard hex codes** are published in IPCC/IEA reports; colors above are sampled/approximated from figures.  
- **Consistency**: Fossil fuels usually dark (black/grey/brown), renewables bright (yellow/blue/green), nuclear/hydrogen special (yellow/gold/light blue).  
- **Aggregated categories**: Some reports group “renewables” without solar/wind separation.
